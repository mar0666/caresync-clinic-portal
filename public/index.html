CareSync Patient Privacy Notice

CareSync Clinic Portal protects patient confidentiality through encrypted password hashing, HTTP-only session cookies, and strict role-based data isolation. Patients can only view, reschedule, or cancel their own records. Authorized clinic staff can view clinic-wide schedules to coordinate patient care.
